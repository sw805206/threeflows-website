Phase 1 — research & validation
1 Conduct market research for the product or service Use AI but validate with industry experts. Focus on market size, growth trend, and top 3 competitors.
2 Conduct your and your team's SWOT analysis Understand "why I/we can be successful."
3 Identify your target customer segment and buyer persona Be specific — "women 25–40 who shop on Amazon" beats "everyone."
4 Build unit economics model For mature-stage profitability planning. See CA004 — Unit economics calculator (coming soon)
5 Conduct a deep-dive 5-year P&L and cash flow projection For small biz, cashflow is #1 critical.
6 🚩 Go / No-Go gate 1 No-go = financially too hard to be profitable within the target time frame and investment.
Phase 2 — MVP & customer validation
7 Develop your MVP product or service Use the MVP to collect long-term cost assumptions. Speed of learning matters more than polish.
8 Conduct voice of customer survey Both survey and with real product. See CK003 — Voice of customer (coming soon)
9 Analyze VOC results and identify top 3 product/service adjustments Prioritize changes with highest impact on willingness to pay.
10 Finalize launch-ready product/service design Collect final cost data from supplier before locking design.
11 Set product/service prices and sales & marketing strategy Price anchoring matters — know your competitors' price points before setting yours.
12 Redo the financial P&L and cashflow projection With revised & validated costs, expenses, and revenue projections.
13 🚩 Go / No-Go gate 2 No-go = MVP and VOC assumptions do not support positive cashflow within target time/budget.
Phase 3 — setup & launch prep
14 Pick a legal structure and register the entity See CK004 — Company & brand setup (coming soon)
15 Secure launch-phase service providers Legal, accounting, compensation, logistics, etc.
16 Set up core business systems (accounting, inventory, comms) See CK005 — Set up your back office (coming soon)
17 Build website and start soft marketing Soft marketing = SEO, social presence, email list — not paid ads yet.
18 Build launch playbook and timeline See CK006 — Launch playbook (coming soon)
