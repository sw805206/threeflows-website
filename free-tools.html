Checklists

Pre-Launch Planning
  • Validate the idea with 20+ target customer interviews
  • Build a unit-economics model with realistic landed cost
  • Define your minimum viable assortment (3–5 SKUs)
  • Pick a legal structure and register the entity
  • Open a business bank account and accounting tool
  • Set a 12-month operating budget you can defend
Preview only
Sourcing & Production
  • Shortlist 3–5 suppliers and audit each one
  • Order paid samples and test against spec
  • Negotiate MOQ, lead time, and payment terms in writing
  • Lock packaging, labeling, and barcodes
  • Confirm certifications and import compliance
  • Place first PO with a clear QC checkpoint
Preview only
Go-to-Market & Launch
  • Choose your launch channel (marketplace vs DTC)
  • Set up storefront, listings, and product photography
  • Wire up payments, shipping, and tax
  • Plan inventory in/out and a fulfillment workflow
  • Draft your first 30 days of content and ads
  • Define the metrics you'll watch in week one
