FAQ
Common questions
Why are order management and fulfillment always bundled? +
Because they're two halves of the same process. An order that isn't tracked through to fulfillment creates blind spots — you won't know if something went wrong until a customer complains. We manage both together so there's a single accountable party for the complete order-to-delivery cycle.
How does Three Flows handle customer service within my brand voice? +
During onboarding, we build a brand voice and response guidelines document together — covering tone, common scenarios, escalation triggers, and what you never want us to say. We operate entirely within that document. For anything outside the guidelines, we escalate to you before responding.
What does "CPA coordination" mean — do you do my taxes? +
We don't file taxes — that's your CPA's job and it stays with them. What we do is keep your books clean and organized throughout the year so your CPA has everything they need when tax season arrives. We coordinate directly with your CPA to deliver reconciled financials, categorized transactions, and supporting documentation — so they spend less time on cleanup and more time on strategy.
What happens as I grow and want to bring things in-house? +
We actively support this. When you're ready to insource a function, we help you write the job description, define the handoff process, and train your new hire using the SOPs we've built together. You don't lose the institutional knowledge — it transfers with the role. Your monthly fee adjusts down as services are removed.
How is the ongoing management service priced? +
Ongoing management starts at $500/month for a focused scope (e.g. order management + fulfillment only). Full back office coverage across all five services starts higher and scales with transaction volume. A volume surcharge applies when monthly transactions exceed your plan threshold — thresholds are set at onboarding and reviewed quarterly. All pricing is custom-scoped based on the services you select, your order volume, and your business complexity. Contact us for a quote.
Do I need to have completed earlier Three Flows services to use Service 04? +
No — Service 04 is available as a standalone engagement. That said, clients who completed Service 03 (launch hypercare) transition into Service 04 with minimal onboarding and a continuity discount, because we already have the process documentation, system integrations, and supplier context in place. New clients go through a structured intake interview.
