What these numbers are — and aren't.

These are 2026 published list rates, shown for comparison only. They exclude fuel surcharge, Delivery Area Surcharge (DAS), demand/peak surcharges, Additional Handling & Oversize / Large-Package charges, address-correction fees, dangerous-goods / hazmat fees, and other accessorials — any of which can change which carrier comes out cheapest for your parcel.

Sources: USPS — Notice 123 Commercial Ground Advantage; UPS — Small Business published rates (not negotiated); FedEx — Service Guide. USPS Ground Advantage is capped at 70 lb chargeable weight; packages above that show N/A.
