The problem
Most first-time sellers fail before they launch

For small businesses, every dollar matters. There's no room to absorb dead inventory, a product nobody wants, or margins that don't work at your volume. The entrepreneurs who succeed validate first — and commit capital second.

  • 📦Buying inventory before validating demand — then being stuck with unsellable stock
  • 💸Underestimating real costs: platform fees, shipping, tariffs, packaging, returns
  • 🔍Not knowing if the market is already saturated or owned by a competitor
  • 📉Building a model on assumptions instead of real market data

FAQ
Common questions
How long does the business planning engagement take? +
It depends on the package. Starter reports take 1–2 business days. A Standard engagement typically runs 1–2 weeks. A Comprehensive engagement takes 2–3 weeks depending on product complexity and how quickly we can align on findings. The exact timeline is mutually agreed upon during our initial discovery call before we begin — we won't start the clock until we've both confirmed scope and timing.
How can I reduce the cost and turnaround time? +
Come prepared. The more work you've done before we start — competitor research, product specs, a rough cost target, your target customer — the less time we spend on discovery, and the faster we can get to the analysis that actually matters. Clients who front-load their preparation consistently get more out of their engagement and finish faster. We'll send you a pre-engagement prep checklist during onboarding.
Do I need to have a supplier already? +
No — business planning comes before sourcing. You just need a product idea. If you're curious about rough landed cost, we can include a high-level cost estimate as part of the engagement, but full supplier identification happens in Service 02. The business planning stage is about validating whether the idea is worth pursuing — not who's going to make it.
What if my idea doesn't pass validation? +
That's a success — you've saved yourself from a much more expensive mistake. Our gap assessment will walk you through exactly what the data shows, what would need to change to make the idea viable, and whether it's a matter of timing, positioning, or a fundamental market problem. Sometimes the answer is a pivot; sometimes it's a different product in the same category. Either way, you leave with clarity — not a sunk cost.
What's the difference between this and a business plan? +
A business plan is a document — this is a process backed by real expertise and live data. We produce written deliverables, but more importantly, you have a real expert walking you through the logic behind every number and every data point. We explain the "why" behind the analysis, challenge your assumptions, and help you pressure-test your thinking in real time. No template, no AI-generated market sizing, no boilerplate. Just honest analysis of your specific idea, in your specific category, at your specific volume.
How is this service priced? +
Starter reports start at $20 per report — each is a focused market or competitor scan you can order individually. Core engagements range from $50 for a focused advisory session up to $1,500 for a comprehensive validation package. Clients who return for a second product or an updated analysis receive a continuity discount. All pricing is confirmed before we begin — no surprises.
